I'm on Roatan (Honduras) doing some diving. Four days into the trip, my MBP bricks itself, because of course it does. No warning, no apparent cause, just arrive back at the hotel room where it's been cool and dry and out of the sun the entire time and it won't show so much as a boot logo.

So, for the next couple weeks, I'm a phone man.

A couple observations so far/thoughts on mobile platforms:

- YouTube has become my number one time wasting liability on desktop BY FAR. That said, I can only stomach it because Brave removes ads. A boon of switching to phone-only is that the threat of ads is enough to reduce my watch time from hours per day to 0 seconds per day. Best case scenario: I've entered rehab, going cold turkey, and will be better off for it on the other side.

- Single tasking has advantages. Switching apps has enough friction that I'm focused for longer periods of writing uninterrupted. Apple is so bad at what they do that some of their misfires roll back around to being accidentally good.

- With no other choice, I booked some work flights and hotel on my phone today. With no easy multi-tabbing and iOS' stunted UI, it was exactly as bad as expected. The iPhone turns 20 next year. Any excuse it had for not being as good as PC desktops because it's "new" aged out over a decade ago. Mobile operating systems suck, and that's all there is to it.

- Apple is so bad at all things text that it's actually kind of impressive. Autocorrect's false negative (misses a misspelled word) rate is 50%+. Its false positive ("corrects" a correctly spelled word/name to wrong) rate is also 50%+. Editing text (placing a cursor) — sucks. Copying text — sucks. Selecting text — literally a nightmare experience from hell. Trying to use a modifier key (in SSH) — just lol. The situation is so bad that Apple should have declared a code red years ago. Not a single engineer should be tasked to obscenely decadent projects like the Duo or Liquid Glass until these fundamentals are fixed.

- Never been happier than now with my decision to never, ever use algorithmic black holes like TikTok or Instagram Reels under any circumstances. Overall, my time on the phone is actually relatively wholesome.

I'm experimenting with using ChatGPT for longer text using its built in dictation (Apple's is once again, like all their software, useless). This is working well. It requires editing thanks to my verbal ticks (I say "like" and "kind of" constantly) but ChatGPT transcribes what I said to it with almost 100% accuracy — really, really impressive. So counterintuitively, expect a few more dispatches like this.

My biggest takeaway is how primitive mobile OSes are multiple decades in. We need to stop holding these pieces of crap to a different standard -- there's no reason a mobile OS should be less powerful than a desktop OS. We've all got Stockholm Syndrome at mass scale.

I hate to say it, but Steve Jobs was wrong.
